I've spent my career helping major brands like TUI, Holland & Barrett, and M&S build better digital experiences. Most recently at TUI, I've been leading squads to rethink holiday discovery — establishing the brand as a top authority in travel search by leaning into AI and automation, rebuilding our core page templates mobile-first, and shipping a new static template that drove +6.2% page conversion and +1 SEO ranking. Alongside this, I've led page-level optimisations that have added over £1m in annual margin.

Before that, I was at Holland & Barrett, where I helped start discovery on their brand-new loyalty scheme and led the discovery for features that drove long-term retention and increased total annual customer value by £829k pa. I also spent time at Marks & Spencer, where I focused on making the Sparks program more rewarding — from exploring new premium membership tiers to running experiments that successfully cut checkout bounce rates by 3%.

I actually started my product journey at Interactive Schools, as the Lead Digital Experience Manager, where I managed the launch of over 50 websites annually and built new testing processes that slashed bug reports by 50%. I'm at my best when I'm using data to solve real human problems or taking a product from 0 to 1.
